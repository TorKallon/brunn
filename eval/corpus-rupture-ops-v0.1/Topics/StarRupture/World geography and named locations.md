| Location | Grounding |
|---|---|
| **Landerworks** | Beside the initial lander in the western/southwestern production corridor. Direct base pin visible. |
| **Flowworks** | Selected between Landerworks and Faraday, east/northeast of Landerworks and west/northwest of Faraday. Proposed footprint, not a placed Core. |
| **Faraday Works** | Immediately north of CRRO “Warm Dawn,” east/southeast of Flowworks. Terrain-registered rather than directly labeled in its crop. |
| **Grand Basin Reserve** | North of the active corridor, immediately east of GRO “Mythic Cry.” The marker denotes the basin area, not a future Core. |
| **GRO “Mythic Cry”** | Mountain POI immediately west of Grand Basin, approximately `2518.767,3099.107`. |
| **Forgotten Engine** | Major east-central landmark, northeast of Faraday and east/southeast of Grand Basin. |
| **Sunstone Station** | Known to be near and subordinate to Landerworks; exact pin open. |
| **Brimstone Utility** | Known to feed Faraday; exact pin open. |

The original provisional Grand Basin match to the Red Lake/Redleaf basin was wrong. The canonical site is beside Mythic Cry.

## Direction-language contract

Prefer landmark-relative directions rather than unsupported exact coordinates:

- “Mythic Cry is immediately west of Grand Basin in the mountains.”
- “Flowworks is between Landerworks and Faraday.”
- “The Forgotten Engine is northeast of Faraday.”
- “The safer Goethite triple is northwest of Mythic Cry/Grand Basin.”
- “The preferred Oil site is west/southwest of Grand Basin.”

Do not mix the screenshot registration frame and SRDB map coordinates as if a verified transform exists. Coordinates in planning notes identify SRDB markers; the overview's site pins were terrain-registered visually.
