1. **Post-Rupture caves and western value loop:** use the cave window first, then Warm Dawn → Grey Owl → Lemon Souls → Purple Haze.

Run Redleaf afterward if the Turbine/Electromagnetic Coil blueprint package is still the actual blocker.

## Combat operating model

The adopted Engine plan is low-twitch and defensive:

- use a shotgun for close waves and corridors;
- use an accurate rifle for Flingers, Spitters, and controlled ranged fire;
- use doors, retreats, line-of-sight breaks, and shields rather than standing in open DPS races;
- carry a manual-save safety point and preserve an abort route;
- stage ammunition and healing before entering;
- do not rely on cross-planet live ammunition delivery during combat.

This is a player constraint, not merely a conservative default: the player explicitly described themself as poor at twitch FPS play. Plans should favor preparation, cover, retreat, and resource margin. Never present a recommended weapon or attachment as owned unless the live loadout confirms it.

The specific SLAMS-12, MAR-9, M175, LEM, and mod suggestions in the imported package are recommendations, not confirmed inventory.

## First Geo Scanner completion

The first completed Geo Scanner defense was much harder than expected and ultimately required a full temporary fire base. The first attempt ended in death and a respawn at a previously claimed nearby base. That was geographically useful, but the site had no spare weapons or replacement materials, so recovery still required walking all the way back to Landerworks.

For the next attempt, the Regeneration Chamber was claimed at the scanner fire base, but the turrets had not been powered and did nothing during the assault. The player was overwhelmed again. Infection/corruption then disabled the base and took the Regeneration Chamber offline, so this death returned the player to the starting launcher instead of the intended local checkpoint. This is direct field observation from one encounter; the exact corruption threshold and general chamber-disable rules remain unverified.

The successful attempt used a built-out fire base with all turrets powered. Once properly staged, the defense changed from frustrating to highly satisfying: the player could listen to the turrets continuously engage the wave, described as hearing them “sing” against the enemy. The scanner was completed, loot was being sorted, and exploration continued toward the next major fight.

The site also appeared to yield two particularly good food-related rewards or stacks, possibly involving a quantity of 20. The dictated report is ambiguous, so the exact item names and counts are deliberately unresolved rather than normalized into invented loot.

## Geo Scanner fire-base checklist

- Claim a nearby Regeneration Chamber, but assume it can fail if the local base loses power or becomes corrupted.
- Power every turret and verify that each one actually tracks/fires before starting the scanner.
- Stage at least one replacement weapon kit, ammunition, healing, and basic rebuilding materials at a separate safe cache.
- A claimed remote base without replacement gear is not a complete recovery point.
- Keep the main recovery cache outside the scanner's likely corruption/failure area; do not colocate every fallback with the contested fire base.
- Preserve a route back to a stable production base or launcher, but plan so that walking it is the final fallback rather than the normal recovery loop.
- Treat first-time scanner activation as a deliberate defense event, not a routine map-reveal interaction.

## Grand Basin construction safety

Mythic Cry is a medium-threat infected cleanup west of the basin. Clear infection before placing the nearest district, preserve a POI/cliff exclusion strip, and keep the first Core east of the mountain boundary. Do not activate the Geo Scanner near `3059,2618` while ordinary construction is underway; its defense encounter is separate.
