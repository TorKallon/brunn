Some important things to note that might not have been well described in those guides:

Unlike pure factory games like Satisfactory, this game has oddly bursty dynamics: 1/you often leave the factory and explore, fight for hours at a time. And you need to do this pretty often. 2/there are piles of turn-ins and corp OCL requests that don't build on each other. So often you'll need a pile of one thing and then move on to a pile of other things. It makes large buffers very nice for grabbing things to turn in and also allows even slow production to sometimes work fine because you don't need things quickly and whole parts of your factory would site idle if you provision for constantly needing everything
Transport is PULL and not PUSH based. Things only go onto rails when needed by storage of factories
The heat dynamic encourages spreading things out rather than building densly.
But... rails crossing over/under rails can be harder, especially when you are building on platforms
