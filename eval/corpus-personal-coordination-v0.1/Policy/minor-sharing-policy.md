# Minor participation and sharing policy

`relation:guardian-1201` links minor `person:p-1201` River K. to guardian
`person:p-1202` Dana K. from `source:guardian-form-r2`. The relationship itself
has sensitivity `restricted_family`.

Fact-scoped grant `authority:guardian-1201` lets the guardian control the
minor's participation consent and sharing/export decisions. It does not let
the guardian edit the official event schedule. Organizer authority controls
schedule facts; a family copy has no schedule authority.

Field policy labels `contact_email` (`river.k@example.com`) as `restricted`,
`precise_handoff_place` as `restricted`, and display alias as `shareable`.
Relationship notes and inferred assessments are excluded from public export.
