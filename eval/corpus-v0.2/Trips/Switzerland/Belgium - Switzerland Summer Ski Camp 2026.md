## Belgium camp
Indoor Slalom Camp, Snow Valley, Belgium (August 5-10)
I am offering a four-day slalom technical camp at Snow Valley Indoor Ski Dome in Peer, Belgium. This camp takes place the week before the Saas Fee camp. Once it concludes, athletes will travel directly from Brussels to Geneva to join the Saas Fee group on August 10th.
Snow Valley is an excellent training facility where many national teams and ski clubs go to improve their slalom technique, consistency, and speed. It offers high-volume, highly productive slalom training in consistent, hard, icy conditions. With two 2-hour sessions per day, athletes will have the opportunity to make significant improvements in their technique. Last summer, 21 CMAC athletes attended this camp.
The YouTube video below provides a great example of indoor slalom training at Snow Valley:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lHfdPiy8sac
Camp Includes:
- Shuttle from Brussels Airport to the hotel and back
- Accommodations at Hotel De Watermolen, Bocholt, with three meals per day https://www.de-watermolen.com
- Two 2-hour slalom gate training sessions per day (8 sessions total), including coaching and video analysis
- Supervision and assistance with ski tuning/sharpening
- Afternoon activities
- Transport of one ski bag per athlete (up to 50 pounds) from Belgium to Saas Fee
I will meet athletes at Brussels Airport on August 5th and ensure they catch their flight to Geneva, Switzerland, on August 10th.

## Saas Fee camp
Saas Fee Ski Camp, Switzerland (August 10-22)
I believe that Saas Fee is the perfect venue for the summer ski camp. The cultural experience that comes with this camp is incomparable. Athletes have the opportunity to travel and experience new places. Combined with daily gate training, dryland, and afternoon activities, we aim to create a unique experience that keeps athletes motivated and, most importantly, having fun.
Saas Fee is one of the best locations for summer skiing. The high elevation of the ski area provides consistent training conditions. Various national teams often train on the same slopes as our group, serving as an excellent example of proper ski technique. This camp is intended for serious racers with strong work ethics who are committed to improving their skills.
Ski area website:
http://www.saas-fee.ch/en/Home
Camp Includes:
- Shuttle from Geneva Airport to Saas Fee and from Saas Fee to Milano Malpensa Airport
- Accommodations at Hotel Bristol with three meals per day (http://www.hotel-bristol-saas-fee.ch/)
- Coaching, lift tickets, lane fees, and sports field reservation fees
- Organized afternoon activities and associated fees (water park, high ropes course)
- Travel costs for a day trip to Zermatt
Price: $4,400
- $200 discount available if you do not travel with the team from Geneva to Saas Fee and from Saas Fee to Milano
Price does not include: Airfare and some travel food costs
