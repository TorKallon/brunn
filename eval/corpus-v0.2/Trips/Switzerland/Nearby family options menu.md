## Purpose
Menu of family-trip options connected to the Saas-Fee camp, balancing quick return access with more varied, less mountain-town-heavy experiences.

## Phase 1, while Radley is in Saas-Fee
Goal: keep Jen, Rourke, and Elise within about a 4-hour drive, with strong preference for places that do not feel like more summer mountain-town repetition.

### Best base options
#### 1. Vevey / Montreux
- Approx drive from Saas-Fee: about 1 hour 50 minutes
- Best fit if the priority is a fast return plus a real break from alpine-village mode
- Why it works:
  - low altitude
  - lakefront setting
  - easy, pretty, relaxing
  - lots of cafés, promenade time, boats, and family-friendly sightseeing
- Good anchors:
  - Chillon Castle
  - Chaplin’s World
  - Lavaux vineyards and lake boats

#### 2. Lausanne
- Approx drive from Saas-Fee: about 2 hours 10 minutes
- Best all-around city + lake compromise
- Why it works:
  - more city energy than Montreux/Vevey
  - still easy to get back to Saas-Fee
  - strong food/culture options
- Good anchors:
  - Olympic Museum
  - Ouchy lakefront
  - old town / cathedral
  - easy side trips along Lake Geneva

#### 3. Fribourg with Gruyères / Broc day trips
- Approx drive from Saas-Fee: about 2 hours 20 to 2 hours 30 minutes
- Best food / quirky Swiss option
- Why it works:
  - less resorty than alpine towns
  - strong culture + chocolate/cheese day-trip appeal
- Good anchors:
  - Fribourg old town
  - Gruyères Castle
  - Maison du Gruyère
  - Maison Cailler in Broc

### Strong secondary options
#### Bern
- Approx drive: about 2 hours 50 minutes
- Good if you want a compact city break
- Anchors:
  - old town
  - Bear Park
  - Zentrum Paul Klee
  - Einstein context

#### Geneva + CERN
- Approx drive: about 2 hours 50 minutes
- Best science-heavy option
- Important correction: CERN is near Geneva/Meyrin, not Bern
- Anchors:
  - CERN Science Gateway
  - Geneva lakefront / old town / Carouge

#### Lucerne
- Approx drive: about 3 hours
- Very strong museum-for-kids option, but a bit farther and less directly convenient than the Lake Geneva side
- Anchor:
  - Swiss Museum of Transport

### Wildcards / one-off day or overnight stops
- Interlaken, about 2 hours 35 minutes, definitely in range, but still somewhat mountain-energy
- Gruyères / Broc, great quirky family stop even if not the main base
- Neuchâtel, quieter lakeside alternative if a calmer pace sounds appealing

## Phase 2, after Saas-Fee ends, with Radley
Planning assumption: family meets Radley only after Saas-Fee ends, around Aug 21 or Aug 22, then does 3 to 4 days in Italy.

### Best Italy add-on options
#### 1. Stresa / Baveno, Lake Maggiore
- Approx drive from Saas-Fee: about 2 hours 15 to 2 hours 20 minutes
- Best overall Italy answer
- Why it works:
  - close and logistically easy
  - easy handoff if the team stop or exit is near the Simplon / Domodossola / Malpensa corridor
  - not mountain-town repetition
- Good anchors:
  - Borromean Islands
  - Isola Bella
  - Isola Madre
  - Isola dei Pescatori
  - Parco Pallavicino

#### 2. Orta San Giulio
- Approx drive from Saas-Fee: about 2 hours 35 minutes
- Best if you want a slower, charming, food-forward Italy finish
- Good anchors:
  - old town
  - Isola San Giulio
  - Sacro Monte
  - lake day / boats / swimming

#### 3. Lake Maggiore + Milan split
- Best mixed itinerary if pickup logistics stay fuzzy
- Good version:
  - 2 nights Lake Maggiore
  - 1 to 2 nights Milan

#### 4. Milan
- Approx drive from Saas-Fee: about 3 hours 15 minutes
- Best low-friction city option if handoff revolves around Malpensa or Milan
- Good anchors:
  - Duomo rooftops
  - science and Leonardo museums
  - Navigli

### Lower-priority Italy option
- Lake Como is valid, but farther and less convenient than Lake Maggiore or Orta for this trip

## Notes
- Best overall non-repetitive Swiss-side answer is the Lake Geneva zone, especially Vevey / Montreux or Lausanne.
- Best overall Italy-side answer is Lake Maggiore.
- Interlaken is in range, but it does not really solve the “we don’t want more mountain town” concern by itself.
- CERN is absolutely viable, but it belongs in a Geneva / Meyrin plan, not a Bern plan.
