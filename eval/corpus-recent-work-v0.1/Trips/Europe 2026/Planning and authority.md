# Europe 2026 planning and authority

This note is a working plan, not proof of a booking.

The ski-team packet included a coach sample itinerary with example booking
references. Those examples must never be treated as passenger confirmations.
Passenger-specific airline confirmations are authoritative for booked flights.

The intended trip shape is:

- one youth traveler reaches Brussels for camp before the rest of the family;
- the family meets in Geneva after the Belgium leg;
- use a low-altitude Lake Geneva base while the Saas-Fee camp runs;
- finish with three or four days in northern Italy;
- spend the final night in Milan if the confirmed return still leaves from
  Milan Linate.

Open planning constraints include altitude contingency, the camp handoff, and
keeping the Italy finish compatible with the confirmed return airport.
