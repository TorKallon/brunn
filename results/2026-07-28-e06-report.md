# E06 — Resume delta experiment

Status: Complete — negative result; D03 rejected

## Verdict

Keep `resume_deltas` off and do not advance D03 to a Nyx rollout. The
treatment passed every deterministic performance, SQL-count, footprint, and
lineage gate, but failed all three quality/budget requirements that motivated
the feature:

- B produced 0/5 complete transitions cases in all three draws, so it did not
  produce the required first-ever case win.
- B did not significantly improve claims over either control. Its one-sided
  exact claim-level McNemar p-values were 0.8125 versus A and 0.96875 versus C.
- The operation-level resume payload was larger under B in all 15/15 paired
  draw-case comparisons: +63,387 characters total, +4,225.8 per pair on
  average.

The tested candidate is therefore rejected. A future redesign would require a
new predeclaration and experiment; this result does not authorize a targeted
repeat or a post-hoc threshold change.
