# E07 definitive supersession result

## Executive verdict

E07 completed all 162 frozen case-runs. The explicit-supersession retrieval
mechanism passes the predeclared quality, deterministic, safety, and overfetch
gates. D04 nevertheless fails its unprompted adoption gate and is not ready for
Tier C.

- **Mechanism: pass.** The four-case supersession family produced 6 flag wins,
  2 baseline wins, and 4 ties across 12 paired case-instances. Net +4 clears
  the +3 minimum. Flag passed 29/48 family claims versus 25/48 for baseline.
- **Statistical precision: weak.** The case-clustered expected per-draw family
  claim delta was +1.333 with 95% CI [-2.333, 3.667]. Majority-collapsed
  full-case outcomes had no discordant cases and exact McNemar p = 1.0. E07
  passes its frozen net-win rule; it does not establish a precise nonzero
  effect.
- **Safety and overfetch: pass.** The flag introduced no new outside-family
  forbidden assertions and no significantly negative outside-family
  asymmetry. Flag context was 1.00812659 times baseline, below the 1.05 cap.
- **Deterministic performance: pass.** Flag-on foreground write p95 was
  27.401 ms at 64K/30, below 58.0 ms, with all six operations inside the
  independently reviewed query-count contract.
- **Adoption: fail.** Syntactic supersession adoption was 7/18 (38.889%),
  below 50%. A stricter semantic replay found only 6/18 resolvable distinct
  edges, 5/18 selected projected edges, and 3/18 noncompeting selected
  emissions.

The predeclared consequence applies: assisted authoring is required before
Tier-C reliance. Keep the official scorer result frozen; its semantic companion
shows why syntactic acceptance alone is optimistic.

## Conclusion and next action

D04's demotion-and-annotation mechanism is accepted under E07's frozen
mechanism gate. The result is encouraging but statistically imprecise, and the
calendar case remains a chronic regression. Do not interpret the mechanism
pass as broad reasoning superiority.

D04 is not deployable as a Tier-C dependency because agents do not author
usable supersession edges often enough without prompting. Keep
`supersession_demotion` default-off. Add the predeclared assisted-authoring
step through `supersession_warnings`, reject self-edges, surface
parallel-successor ambiguity, and run a new adoption qualification. Preserve
this E07 run unchanged as the oracle-seeded mechanism baseline.
