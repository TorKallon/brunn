# E08 — Intention ledger experiment

Status: Stopped at deterministic preflight — no D05 feature verdict

## Conclusion

E08 did not reach an intention-ledger feature comparison. Its flag-on query
calibration recorded all required query-count samples, but failed the
nonintentional 64K concurrent-search regression gate: **874.535 ms p95 against
the 750.0 ms ceiling**. The separate
`query_budget_calibration_is_not_acceptance` failure is intentional for every
calibration artifact.

The frozen protocol requires a wholly acceptable calibration before a reviewer
may author the flag-on query-budget contract. Because this artifact had the
additional regression failure, no contract could be authored. Neither latency
contrast ran, and no reasoning arm, draw, audit, or aggregate ran. Therefore:

- E08 has no pass/fail feature verdict.
- D05 has no ship/kill verdict.
- `intention_ledger` remains default-off and enablement is not authorized.
- A rerun requires a prospectively approved amended protocol; the frozen
  protocol must not simply be rerun.

## Scope of the non-result

No `e08-intention-ledger` query-budget contract exists. Zero of the two
latency contrast arms, 15 planned reasoning invocations, 129 planned case-runs,
three paired draws, draw-1 abort audit, final audit, or aggregates ran.
Consequently the experiment says nothing definitive about prospective claim

stopped E08 into a verdict.

## Contention diagnostic — not an invalidation

The calibration was launched through a PTY. Docker Compose emitted
approximately 1,697 animated progress lines (approximately 33,000 tool-output
tokens); afterward the Codex renderer was observed near 97% CPU and Nyx load
was 9.99 / 8.44 / 6.34. A separate same-revision E07 non-PTY calibration and
acceptance recorded concurrent-search p95 values of 587.546 ms and 580.013 ms.

This makes PTY/renderer contention a plausible diagnostic. It does **not**
prove causation and does not invalidate, discard, or relabel the E08 artifact.
The exact red result remains load-bearing.
